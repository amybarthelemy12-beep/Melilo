# Melilo

Apache 2.0 language model that translates legal text into plain English.

## Disclaimer

**Melilo is not a lawyer.** Outputs are educational summaries of legal text in
plain language. They are not legal advice and do not create an attorney-client
relationship. Do not rely on Melilo's output for any legal decision. For
matters with legal consequences, consult a licensed attorney in the relevant
jurisdiction.

## Model details

- **Base model**: `allenai/OLMo-2-0425-1B` (Apache 2.0)
- **Fine-tuning method**: supervised fine-tuning on (legal_text, plain_english) pairs
- **Pair generator**: `allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Instruct` (Apache 2.0)
- **License**: Apache 2.0
- **Languages**: English

## Intended use

- Helping non-lawyers understand the gist of legal documents (contracts,
  terms of service, statutes, court filings).
- Drafting plain-language summaries for review by humans.

## Out of scope

- Producing legal advice, predictions about case outcomes, or jurisdiction-
  specific guidance.
- Translating between human languages.
- Use as the sole basis for any decision with legal or financial consequences.

## Training data

Pairs are generated by prompting Olmo-3-7B-Instruct on legal source documents
ingested from a Cloudflare R2 bucket. Each pair record stores the source URI,
source text, plain-English translation, translator model id, prompt version,
and timestamp.

The system prompt instructs the translator to preserve obligations, rights,
conditions, and exceptions; restate legal terms in everyday words; and avoid
adding advice or facts not present in the source.

## Limitations and known risks

- **Hallucination of legal effect**: the model may rephrase a term in a way
  that subtly changes its legal meaning. Treat all output as a draft.
- **Jurisdictional gaps**: legal terms have different meanings across
  jurisdictions; the model does not reliably distinguish them.
- **Source quality**: errors in OCR or PDF extraction propagate into outputs.
- **Distillation artifacts**: as a 1B student of a 7B teacher, Melilo will
  produce shorter, less nuanced rewrites than the teacher in edge cases.
